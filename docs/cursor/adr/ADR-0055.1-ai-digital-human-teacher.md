# ADR-0055.1 — AI Digital Human Teacher (ADHT)

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**PR:** #55 AI Teacher Engine (architecture extension)  
**Depends on:** ADR-0055 (ATE), ADR-0049 (ILE)  
**Amends:** ADR-0055 §2/§7 — clarifies that **architecture** for digital humans, voice, and provider ports is in scope; **live** avatar video / TTS / STT SDKs remain out of scope until Media activation.

## Context

Success OS aims to build a world-class AI Digital Human Teacher Platform: the student should feel they are learning from a real teacher, not a chatbot. The teacher is the center of the platform. Simultaneously, ADR-0055 forbids shipping avatars, animation systems, AI videos, or live classrooms inside the ATE foundation PR.

## Decision

1. Introduce **AI Digital Human Teacher (ADHT)** as a presence & configuration layer on top of ATE — not a second pedagogy engine.
2. ATE remains responsible for conversation intents, reasoning, student memory grounding, curriculum safety, and ILE routing.
3. ADHT owns:
   - Digital-human presence contracts (speech, gaze, gesture, whiteboard, emotional educational reaction)
   - Localized teacher profiles (country appearance/accent/cultural style) — **admin-configurable**
   - Age-appropriate stage defaults (early childhood → university) — **admin-assignable**
   - Personality projection over ATE memory (name, style, weaknesses/strengths, goals, preferred examples, confidence)
   - Provider-agnostic AI service ports (OpenAI, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Azure/Google Speech, Tavus, HeyGen, LiveKit, LangGraph, vector DB, Knowledge Graph)
4. All provider ports default to `enabled: false` / `architecture_ready`. Invoking a port **refuses** live SDK calls rather than faking success.
5. Knowledge answers still flow only through verified curriculum, Knowledge Graph, digital books, platform resources, and official references — via ATE grounding. Uncertainty must be stated.
6. Example profiles (JO, EG, US, JP) are **seeds**, not hardcoded platform teachers. Admins may add/edit/disable freely.
7. Live camera, virtual labs, and AR/VR remain future multimodal kinds.

## Consequences

- Product can demo Digital Human architecture and admin configuration today without pretending Tavus/ElevenLabs are live.
- Media / Learning Intelligence waves activate providers by flipping bindings and adding real adapters — without redesigning Success OS.
- Conflict with “no avatars in #55” is resolved: **no live avatar implementation**; **yes architecture contracts**.

## Alternatives rejected

- Shipping fake live avatar/TTS responses marked as production.
- Hardcoding a single Jordanian (or any country) digital teacher as the only platform identity.
- Bypassing ATE grounding with unconstrained OpenAI/Gemini chat.
