# SUCCESS OS — Master Development Directive

Status: **Binding architecture and quality constitution**

## Immutable architecture
The current platform architecture is final. Development must never redesign, reorder, rename, replace, or break existing portals, dashboards, pages, navigation, routes, or working features. New work may only enrich, optimize, validate, and complete the existing system through compatible additions.

## Quality before speed
Research before generation. Validate before publication. Accuracy, traceability, accessibility, performance, and academic review take priority over volume or speed. Generated content remains a draft until it passes automated checks and authorized academic review.

## Global curriculum model
The knowledge system scales by country → authority/ministry → curriculum → qualification → stage → grade/year → subject → unit → lesson → learning outcome. It supports national curricula and international, vocational, university, professional, and lifelong learning pathways without structural redesign.

## Complete lesson contract
The target lesson package includes recorded/live learning when available, AI teacher/tutor/mentor, objectives, outcomes, summaries, notes, illustrations, diagrams, simulations, definitions, equations, rules, examples, solved examples, practice, homework, exercises, flashcards, mind maps, key takeaways, common mistakes, FAQs, chat, printable/downloadable notes, teacher/parent notes, accessibility, study time, difficulty, prerequisites, related learning, and career/university/professional connections.

## Assessment contract
Lessons support quick, practice, adaptive, timed, homework, challenge, and revision quizzes. Units support tests, revision, summaries, flashcards, mind maps, and AI review. Subjects support midterms, finals, comprehensive practice, mocks, adaptive exams, analysis, feedback, recommendations, weakness, progress, and mastery reporting.

## Video contract
The target is one-click playback with adaptive streaming, resume position, chapters, timestamps, bookmarks, transcripts, subtitles, multilingual audio, playback speed, cross-device sync, accessibility, and offline access when enabled. A script, storyboard, or narration must never be labeled as a finished recorded video.

## Publication gate
No lesson is published until it has curriculum identity, outcomes, complete sections, checked assessment, source provenance, duplicate screening, academic review, accessibility metadata, and a passing quality report. Incomplete content remains `draft_pending_academic_review`.

## Scale and performance
All additions must support semantic search, caching, efficient indexing, mobile/tablet/desktop, low bandwidth, accessibility, high traffic, multilingual delivery, and global scale without structural redesign.

## Official platform teachers (Sara & Ali) — world-class phase
Platform success depends on سارة and علي only. Do not build new teachers until they are world-class professional digital teachers (not avatars). Any subject/book/curriculum/university/course must be teachable via the same Human Engine without rewrite. Sara stays calm/encouraging/organized/gradual; Ali stays direct/practical/analytical — across subjects and languages. Every motion needs an educational reason. They are incomplete until multi-subject acceptance (math, physics, chemistry, biology, languages, programming) passes for both teachers under dual acceptance (agent + owner live Demo after every improvement). Binding: `docs/cursor/platform-teachers-doctrine.md`.

This directive supersedes implementation convenience and is enforced by validation and review status.
